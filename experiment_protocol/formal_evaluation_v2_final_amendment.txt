FORMAL EVALUATION V2 - FINAL DATASET AMENDMENT
================================================

Primary evaluation design
-------------------------
7 controlled scenarios
x 2 supervisor methods
x 1 protocol-compliant trial
= 14 primary formal trials

The completed controlled trials are used as the final evaluation dataset.
No repeated trials are claimed.

Primary endpoint
----------------
Correct release decision:
TP / TN / FP / FN

Trial inclusion rule
--------------------
One protocol-compliant trial is retained for each method-scenario condition.

Trials containing documented protocol deviations are retained in the raw
dataset but excluded from the primary comparison irrespective of whether
their final outcome favours the baseline or proposed method.

Formal scenarios
----------------
no_interaction
weak_pull
correct_pull
strong_pull
side_push
reverse_pull
out_of_zone_pull

accidental_contact was removed because manually generating a repeatable
short-duration transient contact was not sufficiently reproducible.

Statistical interpretation
--------------------------
The primary results are descriptive controlled-scenario results.

Because n=1 per method-scenario condition, the evaluation does NOT claim:
- statistical significance between methods;
- confidence intervals based on repeated trials;
- trial-to-trial standard deviation;
- population-level success probabilities.

The evaluation may report:
- confusion matrices;
- descriptive decision accuracy;
- observed TP/TN/FP/FN outcomes;
- recorded release latency;
- recorded force and alignment profiles;
- computational processing time;
- qualitative and time-series failure-case analysis.

Traceability
------------
Original trial IDs and original files are retained unchanged.
The PILOT01 text contained in historical filenames is not altered.
formal_trial_selection_v2.csv defines which trials comprise the final
evaluation dataset.

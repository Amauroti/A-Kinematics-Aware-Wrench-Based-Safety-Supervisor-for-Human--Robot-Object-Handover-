FORMAL EVALUATION V2 - DESCRIPTIVE SUMMARY
==========================================

Evaluation design: 7 scenarios x 2 methods x 1 retained trial = 14 controlled trials.

Baseline:
TP=2, TN=2, FP=3, FN=0
Scenario-level decision accuracy=57.1%
Balanced accuracy=0.700
Mean trial decision processing=0.647 ms

Proposed:
TP=2, TN=5, FP=0, FN=0
Scenario-level decision accuracy=100.0%
Balanced accuracy=1.000
Mean trial decision processing=0.661 ms

Key observed distinction:
Baseline produced false-positive releases during side_push, reverse_pull and out_of_zone_pull.
The proposed supervisor rejected all three of these unsafe interaction scenarios.

Important limitation:
One retained trial was used per method-scenario condition. The results are therefore descriptive controlled-scenario observations and should not be presented as statistically significant population-level estimates.
